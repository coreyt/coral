# Understanding privacy and storage

Coral keeps ordinary document work in this browser or in files you save.

## What stays local

Documents, recovery data, workspace sizing and preferences stay local.
Mermaid parsing and conversion to Coral do not use an AI provider.

## Network use

Coral is an online-hosted application; loading it requires a network.
The on-device editing model is now available as an experimental release,
and its performance is alpha-level. Coral does not send diagram requests to AI providers
or load speech models, and composer voice input is not available.
The on-device editing model is off until you turn it on; see
[Understanding the on-device editing model](on-device-editing-model.md) for
its download host and storage.

## Clearing data

The AI deferral does not erase previously stored credentials or transcripts.
Clearing site data removes Coral recovery data, settings, older persisted
credentials, transcript archives and cached speech assets.
