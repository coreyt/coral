Every Employee (with name, id, and calculatePay() members) is assigned to a Project (which has a title field) — that's one association edge. Employees belong to a Department (which has a name field) through an aggregation edge, meaning the Department holds employees without owning them outright. One specialization of Employee is Manager, which inherits from it and adds a manageTeam() method; a Manager leads a Department, a separate labeled association edge. At the top of the structure, a Company (with its own name field) is composed of Department, meaning departments don't exist independently of the company.
