This diagram models a content moderation flow with nine components. The User submits a post to the Post Service, which enqueues it for review in the Moderation Queue. The Moderation Queue sends the post to the AI Content Classifier. The AI Content Classifier either flags the post as uncertain, sending it to the Human Reviewer, or auto-approves it, sending it to the Policy Engine. When the Human Reviewer is involved, it submits its decision to the Policy Engine. The Policy Engine either approves the post, sending it to the Publish Service, or rejects it, notifying the Notification Service. The Publish Service also notifies the author via the Notification Service. Separately, the User can file an appeal through the Appeal Service, which re-queues the post for review in the Moderation Queue.
