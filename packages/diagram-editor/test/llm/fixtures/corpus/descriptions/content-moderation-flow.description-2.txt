Nine parts make up this content moderation system: User, Post Service, Moderation Queue, AI Content Classifier, Human Reviewer, Policy Engine, Publish Service, Notification Service, and Appeal Service. The connections: the User submits a post to the Post Service; the Post Service enqueues the post for review in the Moderation Queue; the Moderation Queue sends the post to the AI Content Classifier; the AI Content Classifier flags uncertain posts to the Human Reviewer and auto-approves others straight to the Policy Engine; the Human Reviewer submits its decision to the Policy Engine; the Policy Engine approves posts to the Publish Service or rejects them to the Notification Service; the Publish Service notifies the author through the Notification Service; the User can file an appeal with the Appeal Service; and the Appeal Service re-queues the post for review back into the Moderation Queue.
