Looking at this system from the Customer's point of view: a Customer owns an Account, holds a Card that is linked to that Account, submits a LoanApplication that gets approved as a Loan which is disbursed back to the Customer, and can have a Teller or an ATM process a Transaction against their Account (Account is composed of Transaction instances, and Account generates a Statement). Accounts specialize into two kinds — CheckingAccount and SavingsAccount both inherit from Account — and a SavingsAccount additionally accrues an InterestRate. Stepping back to the institution itself: the Bank is composed of Branch instances, and each Branch aggregates the Teller and ATM instances that work out of it. Altogether there are fourteen classes (Bank, Branch, Account, CheckingAccount, SavingsAccount, Customer, Transaction, Card, Loan, LoanApplication, Teller, ATM, Statement, InterestRate) and sixteen relationships.
