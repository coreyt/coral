Four participants appear: User, App, AuthService, and SMSGateway. The messages, in order: User to App, entering a phone number; App to AuthService, requesting an OTP; AuthService to SMSGateway, sending the OTP code; SMSGateway to User, delivering the OTP via SMS; User to App, entering the OTP code; App to AuthService, verifying the OTP; and AuthService to App, confirming the OTP is valid.
