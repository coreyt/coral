Eight classes make up this domain: Customer, Cart, Order, OrderItem, Product, Inventory, Payment, and ShippingAddress. Customer carries name and email fields. Cart carries an addItem() method. Order carries placedAt and calculateTotal() members. OrderItem carries quantity and price fields. Product carries name and price fields. Inventory carries stockLevel and reserve() members. Payment carries amount and process() members. ShippingAddress carries street and city fields. As for connections: Customer owns Cart, Cart aggregates OrderItem, Customer places Order, Order is composed of OrderItem, OrderItem references Product, Product is tracked by Inventory, Order is paid by Payment, and Order ships to ShippingAddress.
