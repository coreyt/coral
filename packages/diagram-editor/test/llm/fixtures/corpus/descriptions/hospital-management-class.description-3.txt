At the center of a patient's hospital experience sits the Prescription, which is dispensed to the Patient — but tracing backward reveals the whole structure. The Patient books an Appointment, and separately owns a composed set of MedicalRecord entries. That Appointment is conducted by a Doctor, and once it happens, the Appointment generates a MedicalRecord. The same Doctor who conducts appointments also issues the Prescription that eventually loops back to the Patient. Zooming out to the organizational side, every Doctor and every Nurse is aggregated under a Department, and every Department is, in turn, a composed part of the overall Hospital. In total the diagram has eight classes — Hospital, Department, Doctor, Nurse, Patient, Appointment, MedicalRecord, Prescription — connected by nine relationships.
