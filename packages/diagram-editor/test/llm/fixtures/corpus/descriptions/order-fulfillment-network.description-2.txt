Sixteen components appear in this order fulfillment network: Customer, Storefront App, Order Service, Inventory Service, Warehouse Database, Payment Service, Fraud Check Service, Payment Gateway, Warehouse Management System, Pick and Pack Station, Shipping Service, Carrier API, Tracking Service, Notification Service, Returns Service, and Refund Service. The connections: the Customer places an order with the Storefront App; the Storefront App submits the order to the Order Service; the Order Service checks stock with the Inventory Service; the Inventory Service reads and writes stock levels in the Warehouse Database; the Order Service charges the customer through the Payment Service; the Payment Service screens the transaction with the Fraud Check Service and processes the payment through the Payment Gateway; the Order Service sends a fulfillment request to the Warehouse Management System; the Warehouse Management System assigns a pick task to the Pick and Pack Station; the Pick and Pack Station reports the package ready to the Shipping Service; the Shipping Service books a shipment with the Carrier API; the Carrier API provides a tracking number to the Tracking Service; the Tracking Service sends updates to the Notification Service; the Order Service separately sends a confirmation to the Notification Service; the Customer can also request a return through the Returns Service; the Returns Service approves a refund through the Refund Service; the Refund Service refunds the customer via the Payment Gateway; and the Returns Service restocks the returned item through the Inventory Service.
