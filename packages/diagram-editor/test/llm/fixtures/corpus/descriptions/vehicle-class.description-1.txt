This class diagram models vehicles with five classes: Vehicle, Car, Motorcycle, Engine, and Owner. Vehicle has make and model fields and a start() method. Car has a numDoors field and inherits from Vehicle. Motorcycle has a hasSidecar field and also inherits from Vehicle. Vehicle is composed of an Engine, which has a horsepower field and an ignite() method — meaning the Engine's lifecycle is owned by the Vehicle. Owner has a name field and is associated with Vehicle through an "owns" relationship.
