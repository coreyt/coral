This flow ends with a post either published and its author notified, or rejected, and possibly appealed afterward. It begins when the User submits a post to the Post Service, which enqueues it in the Moderation Queue. The Moderation Queue forwards the post to the AI Content Classifier, which makes one of two moves: if it's uncertain, it flags the post to the Human Reviewer; otherwise it auto-approves and sends the post straight to the Policy Engine. Whenever the Human Reviewer gets involved, it submits its own decision to the Policy Engine as well. The Policy Engine then either approves the post, routing it to the Publish Service, which notifies the author through the Notification Service, or rejects it, notifying the Notification Service directly. If the User disagrees with the outcome, they can file an appeal with the Appeal Service, which sends the post back into the Moderation Queue for another round of review.
