An Owner, identified by a name field, owns a Vehicle — that's the one association edge pointing from Owner to Vehicle. Vehicle itself carries make and model fields and a start() method, and is built from an Engine (a composition relationship): the Engine has its own horsepower field and ignite() method. Two more specific kinds of Vehicle exist as subclasses inheriting from it: Car, which adds a numDoors field, and Motorcycle, which adds a hasSidecar field.
