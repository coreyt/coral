This class diagram models an e-commerce domain with eight classes: Customer, Cart, Order, OrderItem, Product, Inventory, Payment, and ShippingAddress. Customer has name and email fields and has an "owns" association pointing to Cart, which has an addItem() method. Cart aggregates OrderItem, which has quantity and price fields. Customer also has a "places" association pointing to Order, which has placedAt and calculateTotal() members. Order is composed of OrderItem. OrderItem has a "references" association pointing to Product, which has name and price fields. Product has a "tracked by" association pointing to Inventory, which has stockLevel and reserve() members. Order has a "paid by" association pointing to Payment, which has amount and process() members. Order also has a "ships to" association pointing to ShippingAddress, which has street and city fields.
