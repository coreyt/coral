Start from the LoyaltyAccount a Passenger is enrolled in, and work backward through the booking process. The Passenger also receives an Itinerary, which is composed of Reservation instances, and separately makes a Reservation directly. That Reservation is booked on a Flight, is paid via Payment, and is composed of Ticket instances; each Ticket has an assigned Seat and generates a BoardingPass. The Flight itself uses an Aircraft, follows a Route, and aggregates CrewMember instances. The Route connects two Airport records — one as its origin and one as its destination. Zooming out further, every Flight is a composed part of an Airline. In total: fourteen classes (Airline, Flight, Aircraft, Route, Airport, Passenger, Reservation, Ticket, Seat, BoardingPass, Payment, Itinerary, CrewMember, LoyaltyAccount) and fifteen relationships.
