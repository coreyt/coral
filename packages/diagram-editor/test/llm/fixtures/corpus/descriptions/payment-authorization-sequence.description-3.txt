Six components are involved: Customer, Checkout Service, Payment Gateway, Fraud Service, Card Network, and Bank. They are connected as follows. The Customer connects only to the Checkout Service, sending "submit payment details" and later receiving "order confirmed". The Checkout Service connects to both the Customer and the Payment Gateway, sending "authorize(amount, card token)" and receiving "payment authorized" in return. The Payment Gateway connects to the Checkout Service, the Fraud Service, and the Card Network: it sends "check transaction risk" to the Fraud Service (getting back "risk score: low"), and sends "request authorization" to the Card Network (getting back an authorization code). The Card Network connects to the Payment Gateway and the Bank, sending "verify funds" to the Bank and receiving "funds available" in reply.
