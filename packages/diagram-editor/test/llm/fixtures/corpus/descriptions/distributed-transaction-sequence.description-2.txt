The Client's transaction ends in success: it receives "transaction committed" from the Transaction Coordinator, the last of seventeen messages. That confirmation only follows three separate "commit" instructions the Coordinator issues — one each to the Order Service, the Inventory Service, and the Payment Service — which only happen once all three services have voted to commit. The Payment Service's vote commit follows its write of a pending charge to the Payment Database and receiving "write ok" back, itself preceded by the Coordinator's "prepare(charge)" instruction. Working further back, the Inventory Service's vote commit follows the same pattern: "prepare(reserve stock)" from the Coordinator, a pending reservation written to the Inventory Database, and "write ok" in reply. And the earliest branch, the Order Service's vote commit, follows the Coordinator's "prepare(order)" instruction, a pending order written to the Order Database, and "write ok" in reply. The entire sequence is set in motion by the Client telling the Coordinator to begin the transaction.
