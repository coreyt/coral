This sequence diagram shows a payment authorization flow with six participants: a Customer, a Checkout Service, a Payment Gateway, a Fraud Service, a Card Network, and a Bank. The Customer submits payment details to the Checkout Service. The Checkout Service calls the Payment Gateway with "authorize(amount, card token)". The Payment Gateway asks the Fraud Service to check transaction risk, and the Fraud Service replies with "risk score: low". The Payment Gateway then requests authorization from the Card Network. The Card Network asks the Bank to verify funds, and the Bank replies that funds are available. The Card Network sends back an authorization code to the Payment Gateway, which tells the Checkout Service the payment is authorized. Finally, the Checkout Service confirms the order back to the Customer.
