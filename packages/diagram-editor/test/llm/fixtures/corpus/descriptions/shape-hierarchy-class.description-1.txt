This class diagram models a simple shape hierarchy with five classes: Shape, Circle, Rectangle, Triangle, and Canvas. Shape has a color field and an area() method. Circle has a radius field and its own area() method, and inherits from Shape. Rectangle has width and height fields and its own area() method, and also inherits from Shape. Triangle has base and height fields and its own area() method, and also inherits from Shape. Canvas has an addShape() method and aggregates Shape — meaning a Canvas holds a collection of Shapes without owning their lifecycle.
