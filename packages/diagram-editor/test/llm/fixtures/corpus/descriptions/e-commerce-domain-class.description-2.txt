An Order (with placedAt and calculateTotal() members) sits at the center: it is composed of OrderItem (which has quantity and price fields), is paid by a Payment (with amount and process() members), and ships to a ShippingAddress (with street and city fields). Each OrderItem references a Product (with name and price fields), and that Product is tracked by an Inventory (with stockLevel and reserve() members). Stepping back to how the Order came to be: a Customer (with name and email fields) places the Order, and that same Customer owns a Cart (which has an addItem() method); the Cart, in turn, aggregates OrderItem just as the Order does.
