Eight components appear in this transaction: Client, Transaction Coordinator, Order Service, Inventory Service, Payment Service, Order Database, Inventory Database, and Payment Database. The Client connects only to the Coordinator, sending "begin transaction" and receiving "transaction committed" at the end. The Coordinator connects to the Client and to all three services: it sends "prepare(order)" and later "commit" to the Order Service, "prepare(reserve stock)" and later "commit" to the Inventory Service, and "prepare(charge)" and later "commit" to the Payment Service, receiving a "vote commit" back from each of the three before issuing any commit instruction. Each service connects to its own database in the same pattern: the Order Service writes a pending order to the Order Database and gets "write ok"; the Inventory Service writes a pending reservation to the Inventory Database and gets "write ok"; the Payment Service writes a pending charge to the Payment Database and gets "write ok".
