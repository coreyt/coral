By the end of this sequence the Player has reported playback progress to the Analytics Service, the fifteenth and final message. Just before that, the Player rendered a video frame back to the Viewer, and before that it reported a buffering event to the Analytics Service. That buffering-event report follows the Player requesting a second segment from the CDN Edge Node and getting the segment data back as a cache hit. Earlier still, the Player had reported to the Analytics Service that playback started, right after its first segment request completed — a request that, unlike the second, was a cache miss: the CDN Edge Node had to fetch the segment from the Origin Server, receive the segment data, and only then hand it to the Player. Before any segment could be requested, though, two setup steps had to happen: the Player got a DRM license from the License Server (which replied that the license was granted), and before that, the Player got the manifest of available renditions from the Manifest Service. The very first step was the Viewer pressing play in the Player.
