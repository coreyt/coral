This class diagram models a company's staffing structure with five classes: Company, Department, Employee, Manager, and Project. Company has a name field and is composed of Department, which also has a name field. Department aggregates Employee, meaning it holds a collection of employees without owning their lifecycle. Employee has name, id, and calculatePay() members. Manager inherits from Employee and adds a manageTeam() method; Manager also has a "leads" association pointing to Department. Employee has an "assigned to" association pointing to Project, which has a title field.
