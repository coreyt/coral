This class diagram models a hospital management system with eight classes: Hospital, Department, Doctor, Nurse, Patient, Appointment, MedicalRecord, and Prescription. A Hospital is composed of Department instances (composition), meaning departments cannot exist independently of the hospital. Each Department aggregates Doctor instances and separately aggregates Nurse instances (both aggregation relationships), so doctors and nurses are associated with departments but can exist outside of them. A Patient books an Appointment. A Doctor conducts an Appointment. Each Appointment generates a MedicalRecord. A Doctor issues a Prescription. A Patient is composed of MedicalRecord instances (composition), reflecting that medical records belong to the patient. Finally, a Prescription is dispensed to a Patient.
