This airline reservation diagram contains fourteen classes: Airline, Flight, Aircraft, Route, Airport, Passenger, Reservation, Ticket, Seat, BoardingPass, Payment, Itinerary, CrewMember, and LoyaltyAccount. An Airline is composed of Flight instances. A Flight uses an Aircraft and a Flight follows a Route. A Route has two separate associations to Airport: one labeled "origin" and one labeled "destination". A Passenger makes a Reservation. A Reservation is booked on a Flight. A Reservation is composed of Ticket instances. A Ticket has an assigned Seat, and a Ticket generates a BoardingPass. A Reservation is paid via Payment. A Passenger receives an Itinerary, and an Itinerary is composed of Reservation instances. A Flight aggregates CrewMember instances. Finally, a Passenger is enrolled in a LoyaltyAccount.
